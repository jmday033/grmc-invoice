GRMC Invoice

GRMC Invoice is a personal, single-user tool. Once per pay period it reads the owner's shift assignments from a work schedule in Google Sheets (read-only), totals the hours, builds an invoice PDF, and saves it as a draft in the owner's own Gmail account. It never sends email; the owner reviews and sends each draft manually. It is not offered to, or usable by, anyone else.
